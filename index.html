Our story

Founded in Houston. Still led by our founder.

Sue Burnett founded Burnett Specialists in Houston in 1974 and still serves as President today. What began as one office is now one of the largest staffing and recruiting firms in Texas, with specialty recruiters serving every major market in the state.

Burnett Specialists is a certified woman owned business and an employee owned firm: the largest employee owned staffing firm in Texas and the second largest in the United States. When you call, you reach people with a personal stake in the outcome. Our leadership team has an average tenure of more than 25 years.

Sue has been recognized as an Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year, a Houston Business Journal Most Admired CEO, and one of the Global Power 150 Women in Staffing by Staffing Industry Analysts.
